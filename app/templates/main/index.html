Their story

Society told us to suffer quietly. We decided to speak.

Bloom Anyway was built by two women who refused to stay silent — one rebuilding through divorce and custody, the other building an online life after leaving abuse. Different roads. The same refusal to wait for permission.

Bloom Anyway exists because we know we are not the only ones. This is a space to heal in community — and to build something that is yours while you do.

Ayesha

Co-founder · Healing

Ayesha left her marriage with nothing and rebuilt from a minimum-wage job, studying through the nights to earn her certification, all while fighting a years-long custody battle. Better jobs followed, until she was finally stable. Today she helps other women navigate divorce, separation, and custody with someone who's actually been there.

Saman

Co-founder · Building

Saman left an abusive marriage and moved in with her mom, then a year later got her own place and started sharing her story online to inspire other women to leave toxic relationships. She grew her platform to 150,000 followers in seven months by documenting her journey — and today teaches women how to become financially independent by building online businesses through digital products and content creation.
